DID YOU KNOW?

Pondicherry was the capital of French India.

It remained under French control for nearly 300 years, which is why the architecture and street grids still feel distinctly European today.

The "Kizhur Vote" decided its fate.

In 1954, 170 out of 178 elected representatives voted to merge with the Indian Union, officially ending French colonial rule.

Arikamedu was a major Roman trade hub.

Archaeologists have found Roman amphorae, glassware, and coins here, proving that trade between India and Rome flourished 2,000 years ago.

The French Quarter

White Town

Characterized by mustard-yellow colonial villas, tall arched gates, and bougainvillea-lined streets. It was originally reserved for French settlers.

The Tamil Quarter

Black Town

Located south of the canal, this area is home to vibrant Tamil culture, traditional courtyard houses (veedus), and historic temples.

The Promenade

Rock Beach

A 1.5 km stretch along the Bay of Bengal. The road is closed to traffic every evening, turning it into a massive pedestrian promenade.
